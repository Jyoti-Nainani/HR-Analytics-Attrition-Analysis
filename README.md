# HR-Analytics-Attrition-Analysis
An Attrition Analysis of Employee Dataset of a company.
## The KPIs: ##

- Total Number of Employees: 1480
- Attrition numbers: 238
- Attrition Rate: 16.08%
- Average Tenure of an Employee: 7 years
- Average Age of workforce: 36.92 year
- Average Monthly Income: 6.50K

## Attrition: ##
In Human Resource, Attrition is the gradual, natural reduction of a company's workforce when employees leave and their roles are left intentionally vacant rather than being immediately refilled.
Employees depart through resignation, retirement, personal reasons, or layoffs, and the company chooses not to hire replacements to slowly decrease overall staff size.
## Types of Attrition: ##

- Voluntary Attrition: Workers choose to quit on their own due to better pay, burnout, or career growth.
- Involuntary Attrition: The company cuts jobs through layoffs, position eliminations, or terminations.
## Key Insights: ##

- Overall attrition is higher in male workforce in 36-45 age group, majorly belonging to Research and Development and Sales Department, despite of higher salary slab of up to 5k.
- Attrition is higher for both male and female workforce for 1st year in company, gradually decreasing till 10 years of tenure.
- Employees from HR, Technical degree and marketing background have higher share in overall attrition.
- Higher Distance from home to workplace impacts and results in higher attrition, especially among female workforce.
- Laboratory Technician, Sales Executive, Research Scientist job role see higher attrition, even in entry level jobs.
- Attrition rate is higher in employees who travel rarely.
